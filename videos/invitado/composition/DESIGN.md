# Observatorio pase

## Style Prompt
White institutional page. Navy type, one magenta action, long holds. The interface is the Observatorio light theme.

## Colors
- Canvas: #ffffff
- Ink: #16284f
- Secondary ink: #4b5563
- Accent: #e8186a
- Button: #b8104f
- Surface: #f7f7f8
- Line: #e5e7eb
- On accent: #ffffff

## Typography
- Display: Archivo
- Body: Raleway

## What NOT to Do
- Do not use a QR.
- Do not invent a second headline.
- Do not list the profile fields.
- Do not cover the type with a full-frame gradient.
- Do not use a real person's email.
